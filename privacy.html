Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

BELDISCO ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your data.

BELDISCO is a non-commercial project. There are no ads, no in-app purchases and no subscriptions, and we don't use analytics or tracking tools of any kind. We don't sell your data and never will.

1. Information We Collect

When you create an account and use BELDISCO, we collect:

  • Account information: email address, username, and display name
  • Profile information: avatar image, bio, favorite genres and other profile details you choose to provide
  • Diary entries: albums you log, ratings, reviews, track ratings, listening dates, and albums you mark as currently listening
  • Live shows: concerts you log, with your ratings and notes
  • Lists: your lists and your want-to-listen albums
  • Social data: who you follow, likes, and comments
  • Images you upload: covers for albums you add yourself, or for albums that have no artwork. These are public and shown to every user who opens that album.
  • Spotify import (optional): if you connect Spotify, we read your saved albums and recently played albums so you can import them. Your Spotify login stays on your device in its secure storage. We only keep the album list you import.
  • Push notification token: a device token used to send you notifications (only if you grant permission)

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and operate the BELDISCO service
  • Display your profile and diary to other users
  • Send notifications about activity on your account (follows, likes, comments) and reminders you can turn off
  • Suggest albums based on what you and the people you follow log

We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

3. Third-Party Services

BELDISCO uses the following third-party services:

  • Supabase: database, authentication, file storage, and backend infrastructure. Your account data is stored on Supabase servers. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Deezer API: album and artist information, cover art, and 30-second previews. Your device connects to Deezer directly to load these, so Deezer sees your IP address, but we never send Deezer your account or personal data. Deezer Privacy Policy
  • MusicBrainz and Cover Art Archive: used to find albums Deezer doesn't carry, and their covers. Your device connects to them directly when you search. No personal data is sent. MetaBrainz Privacy Policy
  • setlist.fm: used to find setlists when you log a live show. Only your search is sent.
  • Spotify (optional): only if you choose to import your library. Spotify Privacy Policy
  • Expo: used to deliver push notifications to your device. Expo Privacy Policy

On this website, the "Notify me at launch" form uses MailerLite to store your email address and send launch updates. You can unsubscribe from any email. MailerLite Privacy Policy

4. Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time in the app (Profile → Settings → Delete Account) or by following these steps. Your profile and all associated data are permanently deleted.

5. Children's Privacy

BELDISCO is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such information, please contact us and we will delete it promptly.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your account and data
  • Opt out of push notifications at any time via the app settings

7. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data, including encrypted storage and secure authentication. However, no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of significant changes via the app or by email. Continued use of BELDISCO after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

9. Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy or your data, contact us at:
